THE CALLING

A burden for people and the Gospel.

Spirit Life Missions Team is built around taking the Gospel of Jesus Christ to people, communities and nations. The missionary role is presented as servant leadership: proclaiming Christ, encouraging believers, raising disciples and serving people with compassion.

The public profile can be expanded with the missionary's verified testimony, conversion story, call to ministry and defining moments as those details are provided.
