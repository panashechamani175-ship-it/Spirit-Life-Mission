WHO WE ARE

Gospel ministry with a heart for people, communities and nations.

Spirit Life Missions Team seeks to proclaim Christ, strengthen believers, raise disciples and serve communities.

MISSION • VISION • VALUES

A ministry with a clear direction.

M

Mission

To proclaim the Gospel of Jesus Christ, reach the lost, strengthen believers and raise disciples who carry the message to families, communities and nations.

V

Vision

To see spiritually transformed people and communities established in Christ and equipped to take the Gospel to others.

P

Purpose

To serve wherever God opens a door through preaching, teaching, prayer, evangelistic missions and community outreach.

Taking Christ to people.

Evangelistic missions, Gospel campaigns, preaching and personal witness are central to the ministry's outward focus.

What this looks like

Outreach planning, Gospel preaching, prayer, follow-up and opportunities for people to respond to the message of Jesus.

Helping believers grow.

Discipleship means helping people move from hearing the Gospel to living it through Scripture, prayer, fellowship and service.

Discipleship pathway

Believe → Grow → Serve → Disciple → Send.

Serving communities.

The ministry sees practical community outreach as a bridge for compassion, relationship and Gospel witness.

Areas of concern

Youth, families, community needs, Christian literature, mission equipment and practical support.

Equipping people to serve.

The future centre is designed to support training, leadership development, conferences, discipleship and mission preparation.

Equipping culture

Train people who can carry the Gospel beyond the centre and into their own communities.
