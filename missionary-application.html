A CALL TO SERVE

Missionary service is more than a title.

We are looking for people who desire to follow Christ, serve others, grow in character and participate in the mission of taking the Gospel to people, communities and nations.

The application helps us understand where you are in your journey and where your gifts may fit. Completing the form does not automatically make someone a missionary; it begins a conversation and review process with the ministry.

✝Christ centredFaith, Scripture, prayer and discipleship.
◎People focusedCompassion, service and community transformation.
↗Mission mindedWillingness to learn, serve and go where needed.
◆Character drivenIntegrity, humility, responsibility and teachability.
